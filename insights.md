# Business Insights

## Regional Performance

The West region generated the highest sales revenue.

Recommendation:
Study successful sales strategies used in the West and replicate them in lower-performing regions.

---

## Category Performance

Technology generated strong sales and profit.

Recommendation:
Increase focus on high-margin technology products.

---

## Discounts vs Profit

Higher discounts showed a negative relationship with profitability.

Recommendation:
Review discounting policies for low-margin products.
